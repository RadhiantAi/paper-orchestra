# General medical research draft

This is a generic manuscript structure, not a journal's submission specification.
Use UK/South African English. Keep the supplied template packages and structure.
Use a structured abstract, Introduction, Related work, Methods, Results,
Discussion, Conclusion and Declarations. A target journal may require merging
Related work into Introduction; confirm its current instructions before submission.

Report only supplied aggregate results. Preserve denominators, units, confidence
intervals and analysis populations. Never invent ethics approval, consent, sample
sizes, statistical significance, authorship, funding or clinical conclusions.
Mark absent information as needing confirmation. Do not include patient identifiers.

Describe the actual study design, eligibility, setting, outcomes, missing data and
analysis. Discuss bias, limitations and generalisability. Select the applicable
reporting checklist separately according to study design; this template does not
certify checklist compliance or clinical validity. Human clinical, statistical
and full-text citation review remain necessary.

The Word file is an editable review draft. Compare its equations, tables, figures
and references with the compiled PDF before sharing it with co-authors.
