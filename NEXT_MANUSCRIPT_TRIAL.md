# Offline trial on a second manuscript

Public procedure, updated 15 November 2026. Use a private project folder outside
the repository. Live model calls remain disabled. This is a proposed next trial;
it has not been run on another real manuscript yet.

## Select the paper and reference material

Prefer a completed or published paper with a trusted final version, at least two
earlier drafts and a co-author return. This gives the trial an independent comparison
target. Supply its title or a narrowly scoped Drive folder. Collect the original
figures and legends, approved title page, relevant correspondence, aggregate tables
and analysis code/output where available. Include the intended journal's instructions
if submission checks are part of the trial. Record missing material explicitly.

## Run the trial

1. Create a separate project and inventory copies of the inputs, preserving originals.
   Connected Drive/email retrieval can be handled in the host chat, with the evidence
   imported into the project. No production model connector is needed for this route.
2. Establish the version order and reference final manuscript. Record expected
   totals, figure identities, declarations and known editorial decisions before
   comparing the tool's output. Keep uncertain statements as author questions.
3. Register two issued baselines and their returned reviews. Assign decisions,
   exercise a deliberate supersession and verify that unrelated unresolved items
   remain pending. Compare the resulting manuscript against the trusted final copy.
4. Reconcile supported aggregate results and figures. Use a statistical recipe only
   when its stated design fits. Keep unsupported analyses for separate review.
5. Export an encrypted backup with a passphrase entered locally. Restore into a fresh
   folder, then compare source hashes, decisions, review-round links and figure assets.
   Review the storage/retention preview and confirm no files were removed.
6. Rebuild a co-author Word/PDF/ZIP pack after reviewing the relocated evidence.
   Inspect both rendered documents, retained title page and images. Resolve anything
   the source material proves; keep remaining clinical or author questions visible.

## Acceptance evidence

- Every input is accounted for as read, partial, unreadable or missing.
- No invented data, references, author decisions or silently dropped comments.
- Accepted current wording is present; superseded wording is absent or explicitly
  flagged. The history remains available after restore.
- Supported counts agree across text, tables and figures. Clinical interpretations
  and method suitability retain human review.
- Wrong-passphrase and changed-evidence cases fail without clearing review gates.
- The restored project produces a checked pack after fresh required reviews.
- The handoff includes a comparison report, unresolved questions and a review ZIP.
  Nothing is sent to co-authors or submitted automatically.

Use anonymised/aggregate source material. Any new patient-level processing requires
the applicable explicit lawful-purpose authorisation. Do not enter backup passphrases
in chat. Choose the next manuscript before collecting its private sources.
